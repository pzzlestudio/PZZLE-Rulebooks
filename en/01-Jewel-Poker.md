# [01] Jewel Poker
**Version:** 1.2 | **Players:** 2–4 (Supports up to 12+)

Welcome to the world of Jewel Poker, a strategic game of set collection, tactical dice manipulation, and pattern recognition.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Recommended Cards | Cards per Player |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 30 - 40 Cards | 15 - 20+ Cards |
| **3 - 4 Players** | 45 - 80 Cards | 15 - 20+ Cards |

> **Note:** A higher card count will result in a longer, more strategic game session.

### Objective
Achieve the **highest total score** by the end of the game through strategic card hand management and manipulating Jewel Dice values on the scoreboard.

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Starting Hand:** Deal **5 cards** to each player.
2. **Central Display:** Place **5 cards** face-up in the middle of the table.
3. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face-down as a Draw Pile.
4. **Jewel Dice:** Place all **12 Jewel Dice** in a common area (The Supply).

---

## Play Phase
Players take turns in clockwise order. On your turn, you must perform **one** of the following actions. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hand Limit:** You must not have more than **5 cards** in your hand at the end of your turn.

### Available Actions:
1. **Cycle Hand:** Discard 1 card from your hand and draw 1 new card from the Draw Pile.
2. **Market Exchange:** Discard 1 card from your hand, take 1 card from the Central Display, and replace the empty spot with a new card from the Draw Pile.
3. **Deploy Jewel:** Place a Jewel Die onto the Scoreboard (starting with the **blank face**). Then, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it immediately without revealing it to any player.
4. **Swap Positions:** Swap the positions of **two** Jewel Dice already on the board. Then, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down. *(Subject to Swapping Rules below)*.
5. **Adjust Multiplier:** Change the multiplier face of a die by **+1 or -1**. 
    * *Special Rule:* You may change a face from **3** directly down to **1**.
    * After adjusting, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down.
6. **Blind Burn:** Draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down immediately. No player (including yourself) may see the card's face.

---

## Jewel Dice & Placement Rules

### 1. Permanent Placement
Once a Jewel Die is placed on the scoreboard, it **cannot** be removed.

### 2. Global Swapping Cooldown
If a player performs a **Swap Action**, no other players may perform a Swap until the turn returns to the player who initiated the swap.

### 3. Personal Cooldown
A player cannot perform the **Swap Action** in two consecutive turns.

### 4. Slot Capacity
Each slot on the board has a specific capacity indicated by dots:
* **Slot 11 (1 Dot):** Maximum 1 Die.
* **Slot 7 (2 Dots):** Maximum 2 Dice.
* **Slots 0, 1, 2, 3:** Unlimited capacity.

### 5. Initial Placement Restriction
The very first die placed on the board **cannot** be placed in **Slot 0**.

---

## Game End & Scoring

The game ends immediately when the **Draw Pile is exhausted**. The player with the highest total score wins.

### Scoring Calculation

The total score is the sum of scores from each Jewel type:

#### 1. Base Score
The score for each Jewel type is calculated as follows:
> **(Slot Value × Die Multiplier) × Total count of that Jewel type in your hand.**

#### 2. Pattern Bonus (Multiplier)
Check for Jewel patterns in your hand (matching symbols in vertical, horizontal, or diagonal lines).
* **3-in-a-row:** x2 Bonus
* **4-in-a-row:** x5 Bonus
* **5-in-a-row:** x10 Bonus
