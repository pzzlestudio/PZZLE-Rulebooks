# [01] Jewel Poker
**Version:** 1.2 | **Players:** 2–4 (Supports up to 12+)

Welcome to the world of Jewel Poker, a strategic game combining Set Collection, Tactical Dice Manipulation, and Pattern Recognition.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Recommended Cards | Cards per Player |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 30 - 40 Cards | 15 - 20+ Cards |
| **3 - 4 Players** | 45 - 80 Cards | 15 - 20+ Cards |

> **Note:** Using a higher card count will result in a deeper, more strategic, and longer gameplay session.

---

## Game Components
To ensure proper gameplay, please verify the following primary components included in your set:

* **Main Scoreboard:** 1 Board – Used to place dice to determine the value of each Jewel type in specific scoring slots (Included in Starter A and Starter B sets).
* **PZZLE Cards:** 1 Deck (30 - 80 cards) – Jewel symbol cards used for set collection and creating scoring pattern bonuses in your hand.
* **Jewel Dice:** 12 Dice – Specialized dice used to indicate multipliers and positions on the Scoreboard.

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Starting Hand:** Deal **5 PZZLE Cards** to each player.
2. **Central Display:** Place **5 cards** face-up in the middle of the play area.
3. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face-down to form the Draw Pile.
4. **Jewel Dice:** Place all **12 Jewel Dice** in the common area (The Supply).

---

## Play Phase
Players take turns in clockwise order. On your turn, you must perform **one (1)** of the following actions:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hand Limit:** You must not have more than **5 cards** in your hand at the end of your turn.

### Available Actions:
1. **Cycle Hand:** Discard 1 card from your hand and draw 1 new card from the Draw Pile.
2. **Market Exchange:** Discard 1 card from your hand, take 1 card from the Central Display, and replace the empty spot with a new card from the Draw Pile.
3. **Deploy Jewel:** Place a Jewel Die onto the Scoreboard (starting with the **blank face**). Then, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down immediately without revealing it to any player.
4. **Swap Positions:** Swap the positions of **two** Jewel Dice already on the board. Then, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down. *(Subject to the Swapping Rules below)*.
5. **Adjust Multiplier:** Change the multiplier face of a die by **+1 or -1**.
    * *Special Rule:* You may change a face from **3** directly down to **1**.
    * After adjusting, draw 1 card from the Draw Pile and discard it face-down.

---

## Jewel Dice & Placement Rules

### 1. Permanent Placement
Once a Jewel Die is placed on the Scoreboard, it **cannot** be removed until the end of the game.

### 2. Global Swapping Cooldown
If a player performs a **Swap Action**, no other players may perform a Swap until the turn returns to the player who initiated the swap.

### 3. Personal Cooldown
A player cannot perform the **Swap Action** in two consecutive turns.

### 4. Slot Capacity
Each slot on the board has a specific capacity indicated by dots:
* **Slot 11 (1 Dot):** Maximum 1 Die.
* **Slot 7 (2 Dots):** Maximum 2 Dice.
* **Slots 0, 1, 2, 3:** Unlimited capacity.

### 5. Initial Placement Restriction
The very first die placed on the board **cannot** be placed in **Slot 0**.

---

## Game End & Scoring

The game ends immediately when the **Draw Pile is exhausted**. The player with the highest total score is declared the winner.

### Scoring Calculation

The total score is the sum of scores from each Jewel type calculated as follows:

#### 1. Base Score
> **(Slot Value × Die Multiplier) × Total count of that Jewel type in your hand.**

#### 2. Pattern Bonus (Multiplier)
Check for Jewel patterns within your hand (matching symbols in **horizontal or diagonal lines ONLY**; vertical lines are excluded).
* **3-in-a-row:** x2 Bonus
* **4-in-a-row:** x5 Bonus
* **5-in-a-row:** x10 Bonus
