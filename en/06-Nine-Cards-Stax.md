# [06] Nine Cards Stax
**Version:** 1.1 | **Players:** 2–6 (Supports up to 12 players)

Nine Cards Stax is a tactical card game of strategic stacking, risk management, and hidden objectives. Players compete to stack cards on a 3x3 grid to maximize points from their positive target Jewel (Green Card) while minimizing or disrupting penalties from their negative target Jewel (Red Card)—both of which remain strictly secret throughout the game.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Requirements (PZZLE Cards) | Secret Target Cards | Chip Tokens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 30 - 40 Cards | 1 Green / 1 Red | 3 Chips per player |
| **3 - 4 Players** | 45 - 80 Cards | 1 Green / 1 Red | 3 Chips per player |
| **5 - 6 Players** | 75 - 120 Cards | 1 Green / 1 Red | 3 Chips per player |
| **7 - 12 Players** | 105 - 240+ Cards | 1 Green / 1 Red | 3 Chips per player |

> **Deck Calculation Note:** The minimum deck size is calculated at 15–20 PZZLE cards per player. Increasing the deck size extends game length and increases tactical depth.

---

## Game Components
* **PZZLE Cards:** Main deck featuring various Jewel symbols.
* **Positive Target Cards (Green):** Cards indicating which Jewel symbol awards positive points.
* **Negative Target Cards (Red):** Cards indicating which Jewel symbol incurs point deductions.
* **Chip Tokens:** 3 action chips per player, used to move top cards or initiate a Final Round Call.

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Starting Hand:** Shuffle the main PZZLE deck and deal **5 cards** face-down to each player.
2. **Secret Targets:** Randomly deal **1 Green Target Card (+)** and **1 Red Target Card (-)** to each player. The two target cards for a single player must feature different Jewel symbols (redraw if identical).
   * *Important:* Both target cards must remain secret from other players throughout the entire game.
3. **Grid Setup:** Reveal **9 cards** from the draw pile and arrange them face-up in the center of the table to form a **3x3 grid**.
4. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining PZZLE cards face-down to form the central Draw Pile.
5. **Chip Tokens:** Give each player **3 Chip Tokens**.

---

## Play Phase
Players take turns rotating clockwise. On your turn, you must choose and perform exactly **one** of the following two actions:

### Action 1: Place a Card
* Play **1 card** from your hand face-up onto any of the 9 positions in the 3x3 grid.
* Immediately draw 1 card from the Draw Pile to maintain a 5-card hand. (If the Draw Pile is empty, continue playing your remaining hand cards without drawing).

### Action 2: Move a Card (Spend a Chip Token)
* Discard **1 Chip Token** from the game to pick up the **topmost card** from any position on the 3x3 grid and move it onto another position.
* **Requirement:** You may only choose this action if you have **at least 1 card** remaining in your hand.

#### Strict Movement Rules
* **No Peeking:** Once cards are covered, players are strictly forbidden from looking at any hidden cards underneath the top layer.
* **Base Layer Immunity:** You cannot move the bottom-most cards that were originally placed during the Setup Phase (only newly stacked cards on top may be moved).
* **No Same-Spot Placement:** When picking up a top card to move it, you cannot place it back onto the exact same stack.

---

## Game End and Victory

### Game End Triggers
The game enters its endgame phase when **one** of the following conditions occurs:

1. **Complete Hand Exhaustion:** All players have played every card in their hand after the Draw Pile has been exhausted.
2. **Final Round Call:** When the **Draw Pile is empty** and **at least 1 player has fewer than 5 cards in hand**, any player on their turn may spend **1 Chip Token** to initiate a **"Final Round Call"**.
   * The declaring player becomes the first player of the final round.
   * The game continues for exactly one last full rotation, proceeding clockwise until it reaches back to the declaring player. The game then ends immediately (even if some players still have cards left in hand).

### Determining the Winner
Once the game ends, all players reveal their secret Green (+) and Red (-) Target Cards and calculate their net score. The player with the **highest net score** wins the game.

### Tie-Breaking Rules
If there is a tie in net scores, resolve it using the following hierarchy:
1. **Highest Positive Points:** The tied player with more points earned from their Green Target Card wins.
2. **Shared Victory:** If net scores and positive points are both identical, the tied players share the victory.

---

## Scoring System

Only the Jewel symbols visible on the **topmost card** of each of the 9 grid positions are evaluated at the end of the game. Covered cards underneath award no points.

* **Positive Score (+):** Count all instances of your Green Target Jewel symbol visible on the 9 top cards.
* **Negative Score (-):** Count all instances of your Red Target Jewel symbol visible on the 9 top cards.
* **Net Score = Positive Score - Negative Score**

---
**Note:** Once a player uses all 3 of their Chip Tokens, they can no longer perform Action 2 (Move a Card) or trigger a Final Round Call, but must continue performing Action 1 (Place a Card) on their turns as normal.
