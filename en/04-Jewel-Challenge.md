# [04] Jewel Challenge
**Version:** 1.0 | **Players:** 2–4 (Supports up to 8 players with additional expansions)

Jewel Challenge is a tactical game of area control and strategic bluffing. Players compete to occupy Jewel Circles on the board by challenging opponents using the symbols on their cards.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Requirements | Scoring Tokens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 1 Expansion (40 cards) | 12 Tokens per player |
| **3 - 4 Players** | 2 Expansions (60-80 cards) | 12 Tokens per player |
| **5 - 8 Players** | 3 - 4 Expansions (120-160 cards) | 12 Tokens per player |

---

## Game Components
* **Jewel Challenge Board:** Features 12 unique Jewel symbols categorized by Elements.
* **PZZLE Cards:** Decks containing various Jewel symbols.
* **Scoring Tokens:** 12 tokens per player (e.g., coins, colored markers, or double-sided tokens).

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Board Placement:** Place the Jewel Challenge board in the center of the table.
2. **Token Distribution:** Each player receives **12 Scoring Tokens** of their chosen color/type.
3. **Starting Hand:** Shuffle the deck and deal **5 cards** to each player.
4. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face-down to form the Draw Pile.

---

## Play Phase
The game proceeds in turns, rotating clockwise. In each turn, one player acts as the **Caller**, and the others act as **Defenders**.

### Hand Management
* **Maximum Limit:** You must not have more than **5 cards** in your hand at the end of your turn.
* **Minimum Requirement:** You must have at least **1 card** in your hand at the end of your turn (except during the final turn, where players may empty their hand).

### The Caller's Action
1. **The Challenge:** The Caller chooses and announces one of the **12 Jewel types** found on the board.
2. **Commitment:** The Caller places **1 to 3 cards** face-down. These cards should ideally contain the symbol of the Jewel type they just announced.
3. **Drawing:** The Caller draws **1 card** from the Draw Pile. (Note: Only 1 card is drawn per turn, regardless of how many cards were played).

### The Defender's Action
1. **Response:** Each Defender chooses to place **0 to 3 cards** face-down.
2. **Folding:** If a Defender chooses to play **0 cards**, they "Fold." The Caller wins against that specific player automatically.
3. **Drawing:** * If a Defender has fewer than 5 cards, they draw **1 card** from the Draw Pile.
    * If a Defender already has 5 cards, they do not draw.

---

## Resolution and Area Control

### 1. The Reveal
All active players (excluding those who folded) flip their played cards simultaneously. Count the number of symbols matching the Jewel type called by the Caller.

### 2. Determining the Winner
* The player with the **highest number** of the specific Jewel symbols wins the round.
* **Tie-Breaker:** In the event of a tie between the Caller and a Defender, the **Caller** is declared the winner.

### 3. Occupying the Board
The winner places one of their Scoring Tokens on the corresponding Jewel symbol on the board to "occupy" it. 
* If the position is already occupied by another player’s token, remove the old token and replace it with the new winner's token.

### 4. Turn Rotation
After resolution, all played cards are moved to the discard pile. The role of Caller rotates to the next player clockwise.

---

## Game End and Victory

### Game End Trigger
The game enters its final phase when the **Draw Pile is exhausted**. The very next turn will be the **Final Turn** of the game.
* *Special Case:* During the final turn, if the next player in order to be the Caller has no cards left, the role passes to the next person in line who still has cards.

### Determination of the Winner
Once the final turn concludes, players calculate their total scores. The player with the **highest score** is the winner.

### Tie-Breaking Rules
If total scores are tied, use the following hierarchy to determine the winner:
1. **Area Count:** The player with more tokens currently on the board wins.
2. **Final Showdown:** If still tied, the player who had the highest count of Jewel symbols in the final round wins.

---

## Scoring System

Points are calculated based on the tokens placed on the Jewel Challenge board:

| Achievement | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Occupied Jewel Circle** | 1 Point per Token |
| **Element Set Bonus** | 3 Bonus Points |

### Element Set Bonus
If a player occupies all **3 positions** of Jewel belonging to the same **Element** (e.g., all 3 Fire-element Jewel spots), they receive a **+3 Bonus**. 
* *Example:* 3 tokens in one Element = 3 points (base) + 3 points (bonus) = **6 Total Points**.

---
**Note:** During the drawing phase, the winner of the turn always has the priority to draw first, followed by other players in clockwise order.
