# [03] IXV
**Version:** 1.0 | **Players:** 2–4 (Supports up to 12+ with additional expansions)

IXV is a tactical simultaneous-play card game where players utilize decks representing Roman numerals (0-39) to compete for tokens through a unique symbol-based hierarchy.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Requirements | Scoring Tokens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 2 Expansion Boxes (or 1 Starter B) | 29 Tokens |
| **3 Players** | 3 Expansion Boxes | 29 Tokens |
| **4 Players** | 4 Expansion Boxes | 29 Tokens |

> **Note:** Each player requires one Expansion Box to provide their unique 40-card deck.

---

## Game Components
* **PZZLE Cards:** 40 cards per player (Numbered 0-39 in Roman Numerals).
* **Scoring Tokens:** 29 tokens (Coins, beads, or any uniform markers).

### Objective
Be the first player to collect **15 tokens** (the majority) or hold the most tokens when all cards have been played.

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Token Pool:** Place all **29 tokens** in the center of the table (The Bank).
2. **Player Decks:** Each player takes their 40-card deck (Roman numerals 0 to 39).
3. **Mode Selection:** Choose one of the following gameplay modes:

#### [A] Full View (Strategic Mode)
Players have access to all 40 cards in their deck at all times. Keep your hand hidden from opponents.

#### [B] Part View (Tactical Mode)
1. Shuffle your deck and draw **10 cards** as your starting hand.
2. Place the remaining 30 cards face-down as a personal Draw Pile.
3. Whenever cards are played, draw from your Draw Pile to maintain a 10-card hand.

---

## Play Phase
The game consists of several rounds. Each round follows these steps:

1. **Simultaneous Commitment:** Each player chooses **1 to 3 cards** from their hand and places them face-down.
    * *Tactical Note:* You may stack your cards to disguise the exact number of cards played (e.g., stacking 3 cards so they look like 1 or 2).
    * *Requirement:* You must play at least 1 card unless your deck is entirely exhausted.
2. **The Reveal:** Once all players have committed, all cards are flipped face-up simultaneously.
3. **Determine Winner:** Compare the total symbol values of the played cards (see **Hierarchy of Symbols** below).
4. **Claim Token:** The winner of the round takes **1 Scoring Token** from the center.
5. **Draw (Optional):** If playing in **[B] Part View**, players replenish their hands back to 10 cards.

---

## Hierarchy of Symbols
The strength of a player's play is determined by the specific Roman symbols contained within the numbers on their played cards.

### 1. Symbol Strength (Priority)
Regardless of the total sum, symbols follow this absolute hierarchy:
> **Joker (0) > V (5) > X (10) > I (1)**

* **Higher Symbol Wins:** A play containing a higher symbol always beats any quantity of lower symbols. 
    * *Example:* 1 "V" beats 100 "X"s. 1 "X" beats 100 "I"s.
* **Quantity Comparison:** If players have the same highest symbol, the one with the **greater quantity** of that symbol wins.
    * *Example:* "VV" beats "V".
* **Tie-Breaking Order:** If the highest symbols and quantities are equal, compare the next symbol in the hierarchy:
    1. Total count of **V**
    2. Total count of **X**
    3. Total count of **I**
    * *Example:* "VV XXX II" beats "VV XXX I".

### 2. Further Tie-Breakers
If the symbol counts are identical across all types:
1. **Card Quantity:** The player who used **more cards** wins.
2. **Highest Card Value:** Compare the single highest-valued card in the play (refer to the card ID/Value).
3. **Absolute Draw:** If all the above are equal, the round is a draw. No tokens are awarded, and all played cards are discarded.

### 3. The Joker "0"
* **Requirement:** The "0" card **cannot** be played alone. It must be paired with at least one other card.
* **Power:** A play containing a "0" always wins against plays without a "0".
* **Joker vs. Joker:** If multiple players play a "0", the winner is determined by the other cards played alongside the Joker, using the standard hierarchy rules.

---

## Game End & Victory
The game ends in one of the following scenarios:

1. **Majority Reach:** A player collects **15 tokens** (more than half).
2. **Card Exhaustion:** Players run out of cards.
3. **Final Player Standing:** If only one player has cards remaining while tokens are still in the bank, that player receives tokens equal to the number of cards they have left (Hand + Draw Pile), up to the remaining token count.

**The winner is the player with the most Scoring Tokens.**
