# [07] Jewel Pocket
**Version:** 1.1 | **Players:** 2+ (Recommended: 2–8 players)

Jewel Pocket is a fast-paced tactical card game of pattern recognition and alignment. Players manage their hands to form intersecting or straight lines of matching jewel symbols to score points or achieve a sudden-death instant victory.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Deck Size | Starting Hand |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 40 Cards | 5 Cards |
| **3 Players** | 60 Cards | 5 Cards |
| **4 Players** | 80 Cards | 5 Cards |
| **8 Players** | 160 Cards | 5 Cards |

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Starting Hand:** Shuffle the PZZLE deck and deal **5 cards** face-down to each player.
2. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face-down in the center of the table to form the Draw Pile.

---

## Play Phase
Players take turns clockwise. On your turn, you must choose and perform exactly **one** of the following two actions:

### Action 1: Cycle Hand
Discard 1 card from your hand face-up into the discard pile, then draw 1 new card from the Draw Pile.

### Action 2: Score Jewels
Place a combination of cards from your hand face-up onto the table. The cards must form a continuous line of **3 or more identical Jewel symbols** horizontally or diagonally only. (Vertical alignments do not count).
* **Hand Replenishment:** After playing the cards, immediately draw back up to a **5-card hand** from the Draw Pile.
* **Exception:** Hand replenishment does not apply during the final turn of the game.

---

## Game End and Victory
The game ends immediately when one of the following conditions is met:

### Standard Endgame Trigger
The **Draw Pile is entirely exhausted**. Players calculate their total scores from their played cards, and the player with the highest score wins.

### Instant Win Triggers (Sudden Death)
A player wins the game instantly, overriding all other players' scores, if they achieve either of the following milestones:
* **The Dual-Set Master:** Being the first player to successfully complete and play two separate 5-card alignments.
* **The Cross-Alignment Master:** Being the first player to play a single connected layout that achieves a 5-card alignment in two different directions (horizontal and diagonal) simultaneously.

---

## Scoring System

If the game ends via Draw Pile exhaustion, scores are calculated based on the cards used in valid scoring sets:

### Standard Scoring
Each card in a standard valid alignment counts as **1 point**.
* *Example:* If a player has completed two 3-card alignments and two 4-card alignments, their total score is 3 + 3 + 4 + 4 = **14 points**.

### Special Combo Scoring
If a single connected set of cards creates alignments in more than one direction simultaneously (i.e., intersecting horizontal and diagonal alignments), special combo rules apply:
* The entire intersecting card structure is awarded a flat **7 points** total.
* This rule applies regardless of whether the intersecting alignments consist of the same Jewel type or different Jewel types.
