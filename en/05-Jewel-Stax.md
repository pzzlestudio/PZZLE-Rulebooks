# [05] Jewel Stax
**Version:** 1.0 | **Players:** 2–4 (Supports up to 8 players with additional expansions)

Jewel Stax is a tactical "Winner-Takes-All" stacking game. Unlike traditional area control, players compete to occupy the top position of a stack. The player on top claims the total value of the entire stack, making every layer a high-stakes struggle for dominance.

---

## Game Specifications

| Player Count | Requirements | Chip Tokens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2 Players** | 1 Expansion (40 cards) | 10 Chips per player |
| **3 - 4 Players** | 2 Expansions (60-80 cards) | 10 Chips per player |
| **5 - 8 Players** | 3 - 4 Expansions (120-160 cards) | 10 Chips per player |

---

## Game Components
* **Jewel Challenge Board:** Features 12 unique Jewel Circles categorized by Elements.
* **PZZLE Cards:** Decks containing various Jewel symbols.
* **Chip Tokens:** 10 stackable chips per player.

---

## Setup Phase
1. **Board Placement:** Place the Jewel Challenge board in the center of the table.
2. **Chip Distribution:** Each player receives **10 Chip Tokens** of their color.
3. **Starting Hand:** Shuffle the deck and deal **5 cards** to each player.
4. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face-down to form the Draw Pile.

---

## Play Phase
The game proceeds in turns, rotating clockwise. In each turn, one player acts as the **Caller**, and the others act as **Defenders**.

### Hand Management
* **Maximum Limit:** You must not have more than **5 cards** in your hand at the end of your turn.

### The Caller's Action
1. **The Challenge:** The Caller announces one of the **12 Jewel types** found on the board.
2. **Commitment:** The Caller places **1 to 3 cards** face-down.
3. **Resolution:** If the Defender folds, the Caller does not need to reveal their cards and may discard them immediately.

### The Defender's Action
1. **Response:** Each Defender chooses to place **0 to 3 cards** face-down.
2. **Folding:** If a Defender plays **0 cards**, they "Fold," and the Caller wins against them automatically.

---

## Resolution and Stacking

### 1. Determining the Winner
All active players reveal their cards. The player with the **highest number** of the called Jewel symbols wins the round.
* **Tie-Breaker:** In a tie between the Caller and a Defender, the **Caller** wins.

### 2. The Stacking Mechanic (Stax)
The winner places one of their Chip Tokens on the corresponding Jewel Circle.
* **Stacking:** You may place your chip on top of existing chips. 
* **Stack Limit:** A Jewel Circle can hold a maximum of **5 chips**. Once a stack reaches 5 layers, no more chips can be added.

### 3. Replenishing Cards
After resolution, players replenish their hands.
* **Draw back to 5:** Unlike other modes, players draw cards until they have **5 cards** in hand (unless the Draw Pile is empty).
* **Drawing Priority:** The winner of the round draws first, followed by others in clockwise order.

---

## Game End and Victory

### End Game Triggers
The game ends when **one** of the following occurs:
1. All players have exhausted the cards in their hands.
2. All players have used all of their Chip Tokens.

### Special Rules for Final Phase
* **Exhausted Draw Pile:** If the Draw Pile is empty, continue playing with the cards remaining in hand.
* **Exhausted Chips:** If a player runs out of Chip Tokens but still has cards, they cannot place new chips or move existing ones. However, they can still play cards to "defend" a circle they currently occupy (to prevent others from stacking on top of them).

---

## Scoring System

Score is calculated based on who occupies the **top position** of each stack:

| Achievement | Points Awarded |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Chip Occupant** | 1 Point for EVERY chip in that stack |
| **Element Set Bonus** | X2 (Double) points for that Element Area |

### Winner-Takes-All Stax
Only the player whose chip is on the **very top** of a stack earns points for that circle. They receive 1 point for every chip currently in that stack (including those belonging to opponents underneath).
* *Example:* If a stack has 5 chips, the player on top receives **5 points**. The players owning the bottom 4 chips receive **0 points** for that circle.

### Element Set Bonus
If a player occupies the top position of all **3 Jewel Circles** within the same **Element** (e.g., all 3 Water spots), the total score from those three stacks is **Doubled (x2)**.

---
**Note:** In the final turn, if the next Caller has no cards, the role passes to the next player with cards.
